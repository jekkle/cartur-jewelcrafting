# Changelog

## 2.1.0 (Cartur's)

First release of the carried-on Jewelcrafting, from Smoothbrain's 2.0.11 source.

- Dedicated servers: gemstone formations near the spawn area now grow crystals (#129).
- Socket tab shows the real socket cost for items above quality 1 (#114).
- No error when hovering a socketed crossbow with Resonating Echoes, or Pyromaniac (#118).
- "Daring Affects Bosses" off now really leaves bosses alone (#116, #100).
- Gliding no longer lets you pick up past your weight limit (#76).
- New Ukrainian translation; Russian and Chinese brought up to date.
- Wins over Smoothbrain's copy when an add-on installs it, so EpicJewels and others keep working.
